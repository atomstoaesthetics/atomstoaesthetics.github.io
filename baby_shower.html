From 'Me' to 'We' to 'Three'

Last year marked our very first chapter as a married couple. Stepping into that unknown territory was a beautiful, whirlwind experience, and we are so deeply grateful for the blessings and support you showered upon us as we found our rhythm. To our family and friends: thank you for not only standing by us as individuals but for embracing us so warmly as a couple. Your love has been the compass that guided us through our first year of "we."

But as it turns out, the adventure was only just beginning!

This year, we are embarking on a journey even more wondrous and uncharted. We are trading the quiet moments of our first year for the joyful anticipation of a growing family. Our hearts are full as we prepare to welcome our baby boy to this beautiful earth this March.

We wanted to gather everyone together to celebrate this new life and the incredible community he is lucky enough to be born into. This baby shower isn't just about preparing for a due date; it’s about celebrating the love, the art, and the nature of the family we are building with all of you by our sides. We can’t wait to share this day with you as we prepare to welcome our little one home.
